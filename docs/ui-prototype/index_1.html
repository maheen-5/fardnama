Before you continue

Please read this - it explains how your case is handled and what rights you keep.

§
Aligned with PECA 2016Evidence categories and reporting pathways in this tool follow the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016 - so what you prepare here maps to how the FIA Cybercrime Wing actually reviews cases.
🔒
Your evidence is private by defaultEverything you upload is encrypted and visible only to you. No one else , not even FardNama staff , can open it without your permission.
✋
Nothing is auto-submittedWe never send your report, evidence, or location to police, the FIA, or anyone else automatically. Every submission is a deliberate action you take yourself.
🕊
You can delete everything, anytimeYou can export or permanently delete your case data whenever you choose - there's no obligation to keep it here.
🪪
Identified by case number, not nameWherever possible, your case is referenced by ID rather than your name, so your identity stays shielded unless you decide otherwise.
☎
This doesn't replace emergency helpIf you're in immediate danger, please contact the police or a trusted person directly - this tool supports that process, it doesn't substitute for it.
